# Text-Based-RPG-Adventure-Game

A short, interactive, and choice-driven console-based RPG game written in C.

---

## 🎮 How the Game Works

1. You stand at the entrance of a mysterious dark cave and begin your adventure.
2. You make a series of choices that directly affect your stats (**Health Points** and **Gold**):
   - **Scene 1:** Choose whether to sneak into the dark cave (risk a trap and lose health) or build a campfire outside (rest safely).
   - **Scene 2:** Encounter a wild Goblin and choose whether to fight it for gold or run away.
3. At the end of the game, your final health and total collected gold will be displayed!
